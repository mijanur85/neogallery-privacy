Executive Summary: Neo Gallery (com.neogallery.app) is an offline-first mobile photo and video gallery application. We strictly respect your privacy. All your personal photos, videos, and private vault files remain exclusively on your local device. We do not operate remote servers to host, inspect, transmit, or monetize your personal media files.

1. Information We Collect

Neo Gallery does NOT collect, harvest, transmit, or store any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII) on any external server. The application functions entirely offline on your device for all core gallery and media management features.

  • No Personal Media Collection: Your personal photos, videos, and albums are stored on your device only. We never access, upload, or transmit your media to our servers or any third-party cloud.
  • No Account Information: Neo Gallery does not require, offer, or support user account registration, login credentials, or cloud profile storage.

2. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

To provide high-performance gallery and media management features, Neo Gallery requests access to certain device capabilities in accordance with Google Play Developer policies:

  • Photos & Videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO): Required on Android 13+ to display and organize photos and videos stored locally on your device. Media files are read solely for rendering thumbnails, fullscreen viewing, and local management.
  • Selected Photos Access (READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED): Supported on Android 14+ to allow users to grant partial or selective media access.
  • Storage Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used strictly on Android 12 and older devices (with maxSdkVersion constraints) to read device media and save user-edited photos to your device's Pictures directory.
  • Biometric Authentication (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT): Used exclusively to unlock the Private Vault using fingerprint or face authentication. Biometric validation is processed entirely by the secure Android system keystore. Neo Gallery never accesses or stores your raw biometric data.
  • Wallpaper Setting (SET_WALLPAPER): Used only when you explicitly choose "Set as Wallpaper" from the photo viewer menu.
  • Network Permissions (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required solely by Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, and Firebase Crashlytics to display ads for free users, process premium subscriptions, and report technical stability diagnostics.

4. Google Advertising ID (AAID) Disclosure

Neo Gallery complies strictly with Google Play's Advertising ID Policy:

  • Usage: Neo Gallery uses the Google Advertising ID (AAID) via the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK solely for serving non-intrusive ads, frequency capping, aggregated ad reporting, and combating ad fraud.
  • Zero Linkage to Personal Identity: The Advertising ID is never combined with any personally identifiable information (PII) or personal photos.
  • User Opt-Out & Reset Instructions: Users have complete control over their Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time through your Android device settings:
    1. Open your device Settings.
    2. Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
    3. Tap "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".
  • Ad-Free Option: You can completely disable all advertising and Advertising ID processing by upgrading to Neo Gallery Premium.

5. Private Vault & On-Device Encryption

Items moved to the Private Vault are encrypted locally on your device using hardware-backed AES-256 encryption and stored in internal app-isolated storage. We do not possess master encryption keys and cannot recover your password or encrypted files if forgotten. Please note that uninstalling the application or clearing device storage through Android settings will permanently delete local vault data.

6. Account Deletion and User Data Deletion Instructions

Account Deletion Notice: Neo Gallery (com.neogallery.app) does NOT require, offer, or allow user account creation. The application operates 100% offline for photo management without user accounts, logins, usernames, or cloud-based profiles. Because no remote accounts exist, there is no remote account to delete.

Step-by-Step Instructions to Delete All User Data:

  1. Step 1 (In-App & Device Storage Deletion): On your Android device, open Settings → Apps → Neo Gallery → Storage & Cache → tap "Clear Storage" / "Clear Data". This permanently purges all on-device database records, cached thumbnails, favorites, and encrypted vault files.
  2. Step 2 (Complete Removal): Uninstall Neo Gallery from your device. This automatically deletes the isolated sandbox storage directory and all associated app data.
  3. Step 3 (Web-Based Data Deletion Request Without App Installation): If you have ever contacted our customer support team or submitted any inquiries, and wish to request the permanent deletion of your support correspondence and email records, you can submit a deletion request via our dedicated Web Portal at:
    👉 https://mijanur85.github.io/neogallery-privacy/delete-data.html
    Or send an email directly to neogallery.support@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". All requests are processed within 48 to 72 hours.

7. Children's Privacy

Neo Gallery is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at neogallery.support@gmail.com so we can take immediate corrective action.

8. Security Safeguards

We implement industry-standard cryptographic techniques to safeguard private files within the on-device Vault. Communication between integrated Google SDKs (Google Play Billing, AdMob) and Google servers is strictly encrypted via HTTPS/TLS protocols.
