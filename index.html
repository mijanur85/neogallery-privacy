Privacy Policy for NeoGallery

Effective Date: September 17, 2026 • Last Updated: September 2026

Executive Summary: NeoGallery is designed with a privacy-first mindset. All your photos, videos, and private vault files remain strictly on your local device. We do not operate remote servers to host, inspect, transmit, or monetize your media files.

1. Information We Collect

NeoGallery does NOT collect, harvest, transmit, or store any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII) on any external server. The application functions entirely offline on your device for core gallery features.

2. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

To provide high-performance gallery and media management features, NeoGallery requests access to certain device capabilities in accordance with Google Play Developer policies:

  • Photos & Videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO): Required on Android 13+ to display and organize photos and videos stored locally on your device. Media files are read solely for rendering thumbnails, fullscreen viewing, and local management.
  • Selected Photos Access (READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED): Supported on Android 14+ to allow users to grant partial or selective media access.
  • Storage Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used strictly on Android 12 and older devices to read device media and save user-edited photos to your device's Pictures directory.
  • Biometric Authentication (USE_BIOMETRIC): Used exclusively to unlock the Private Vault using fingerprint or face authentication. Biometric validation is processed entirely by the secure Android system keystore. NeoGallery never accesses or stores your raw biometric data.
  • Wallpaper Setting (SET_WALLPAPER): Used only when you explicitly select "Set as Wallpaper" from the photo viewer menu.
  • Network Permissions (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required solely by Google AdMob and Google Play Billing to fetch advertisements for free users and process premium in-app subscriptions.

3. Private Vault & Encryption

Items moved to the Private Vault are encrypted locally on your device using AES-256 encryption and placed in internal app-isolated storage. We do not possess master encryption keys and cannot recover your password or encrypted files if forgotten. Please note that uninstalling the application or clearing device storage through Android settings will permanently delete local vault data.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

NeoGallery incorporates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display non-intrusive advertisements to users who do not have an active Premium subscription. Third-party advertising partners, including Google, may collect pseudonymous device identifiers, diagnostic information, and advertising identifiers (AAID) in accordance with applicable data privacy laws.

  • For more information on how Google collects and processes advertising data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services.
  • European Economic Area (EEA) & UK Users: In compliance with GDPR and Google consent requirements, European users are presented with a User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog to manage consent preferences for personalized or non-personalized advertising.
  • Ad-Free Option: Users can disable all advertisements permanently or by subscription through our Google Play In-App Billing options.

5. In-App Purchases and Billing

All financial transactions, monthly/yearly subscriptions, and purchases are processed securely by Google Play In-App Billing. NeoGallery does not collect, process, or store credit card details or financial information.

6. Children's Privacy

NeoGallery is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our services, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.

7. Data Retention & User Control

Because NeoGallery does not store user media on external servers, you have complete control over your data. All cache, thumbnail indices, and vault files can be deleted instantly at any time by uninstalling the application or selecting "Clear Storage" in your device's Android App Settings.

8. Security Safeguards

We implement industry-standard cryptographic techniques to safeguard private files within the on-device Vault. Communication between third-party SDKs (Google Play Billing, AdMob) and Google servers is strictly encrypted via HTTPS/TLS protocols.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
