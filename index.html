Android · com.neogallery.app · Contact support for app availability.

A place for your library

Browse your photos and videos, organize albums and favourites, and edit images on your device.

A private vault

Keep selected files in a locally encrypted vault. Vault contents are not a cloud backup; restore or export them before uninstalling.

Make it yours

Pure Black and Pure White are free. Unlock Obsidian or Ivory for 24 hours with an optional rewarded ad, or explore the Premium theme collection with an active subscription.

Creative tools

Edit photos and use on-device subject segmentation for background removal and cutout. Initial model downloads may need a connection.

Local media. Clear choices.

No Neo Gallery account is required for your gallery. The app does not automatically upload your photo library or vault to a developer-operated server. Ads, analytics, crash reporting, purchases and model downloads can use third-party online services. Sharing to another app is your choice.

The free version includes ads. Pure Black and Pure White remain free even when an ordinary interstitial is shown after a completed theme-selection session. Premium suppresses ads while the subscription is active; it does not disable all analytics or diagnostics.

Premium, with the details up front

Monthly and yearly subscriptions are available through Google Play. There is no free trial. Review the localized price and billing period before confirming purchase. Subscriptions renew unless cancelled; uninstalling the app does not cancel one. See the full subscription terms.
